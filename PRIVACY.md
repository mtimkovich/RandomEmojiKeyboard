# Privacy Policy for Random Emoji Keyboard

_Last updated: October 14, 2026_

Random Emoji Keyboard ("the app") is a keyboard that inserts random emojis into
the text field you are typing in. This policy explains what the app does and
does not do with your information.

## Summary

**The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**

## Information the app collects

None. Specifically, the app:

- does **not** record, log, or store anything you type or any text that is
  already in a text field;
- does **not** request internet access or any other Android permissions, and
  makes no network connections;
- does **not** use analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking
  services or SDKs;
- does **not** create accounts or ask for any personal information;
- does **not** share any data with third parties, because it has no data to
  share.

## How the app works with text fields

As an Android keyboard (input method), the app can interact with the text field
you are typing in. It uses this only to insert an emoji, send an Enter key
press, or delete characters before the cursor when you tap the corresponding
buttons. All of this happens on your device, only in response to your taps, and
nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

## Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children
under 13.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same location
